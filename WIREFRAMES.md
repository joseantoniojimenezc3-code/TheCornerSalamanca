# The Corner Salamanca — Low-Fidelity Wireframes

## 1. Experience structure

The page follows a single calm narrative:

1. Arrival and desire
2. Point of view
3. Brunch
4. Coffee
5. Summer drinks
6. Signature toasts
7. Gallery
8. Visit us
9. Integrated menu
10. Footer

The alternation between image-led and text-led chapters prevents visual fatigue. Dense information appears only near the end, after the atmosphere and product quality are established.

## 2. Global navigation

### Mobile

```text
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│ THE CORNER             MENU  │
└──────────────────────────────┘
```

The header sits over the hero initially. The wordmark is left aligned and the text menu trigger is right aligned. On open, a full-screen warm-black panel contains four primary anchors—Story, Menu, Gallery and Visit—plus language and Instagram at the bottom. A text trigger is more elegant and understandable than an unexplained hamburger icon.

### Desktop

```text
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ THE CORNER       Story  Brunch  Coffee       Menu  Gallery Visit │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

The wordmark anchors the left; navigation is split into experience links and intent links. The header becomes a quiet cream surface after leaving the hero. It is compact enough to preserve vertical space and sticky so the menu and visit actions remain available.

## 13. Visual rhythm and whitespace

- **Hero to philosophy:** an immersive image is followed by a cream field and generous top padding, producing an immediate exhale.
- **Philosophy to product chapters:** small process images lead naturally into larger food photography.
- **Brunch and coffee:** opposing image/text directions create rhythm without decorative separators.
- **Summer drinks:** a concise full-width pause changes tempo.
- **Toasts to gallery:** a focused single product expands into a varied environmental spread.
- **Visit to menu:** practical information precedes detailed choice; both sit on quiet surfaces with clear chapter numbering.
- **Footer:** the dark field provides the only major tonal closure after the hero.

Whitespace is active structure. It isolates one message at a time, protects the serif typography, and prevents strong food colors from overwhelming the page. Mobile section gaps remain generous rather than collapsing into a conventional stacked feed. Desktop width beyond 1440px becomes margin, not larger text or stretched photography.

## 14. Hierarchy rules

1. Photography creates desire.
2. Display headings establish the emotional idea.
3. Short body copy adds meaning.
4. Labels and captions orient without competing.
5. Actions appear only where intent is clear.

Each section has one dominant element. Equal-size card grids, multiple competing calls to action and decorative copy are intentionally excluded.
